The 2026 Market Average

For a professional, fully insured 4-5 piece wedding band in Ireland in 2026, couples typically invest between €2,400 and €3,200. This usually includes sound, lighting, and performance from the first dance until the DJ takeover.

Cost Breakdown: What Are You Paying For?

Item / Service Why it matters
Talent & Rehearsal You aren't just paying for 2 hours on stage. You are paying for years of craft, weekly rehearsals, and the ability to read a room instantly.
Pro Audio & Lighting We bring over €20k worth of PA and lighting equipment to ensure the sound is crisp (not loud/screechy) and the dancefloor looks electric.
Insurance & Tax Professional bands carry Public Liability Insurance (required by venues like Adare Manor) and are tax-compliant businesses.
Logistics & Travel Fuel, van maintenance, and 12+ hours of crew time from load-in to load-out (often finishing at 3am).

Common Questions

Do you offer a DJ service? +
Yes! Most couples book our "Full Evening Package". This includes the live band performance followed immediately by our professional DJ service. This ensures zero "dead air" between the band finishing and the DJ starting, keeping the dancefloor full.
Can we see you live before booking? +
Yes. We host live showcases in Dublin so you can see us before booking. Reserve a seat here.
Do you charge for travel? +
Our base price typically covers Dublin and surrounding counties (Meath, Kildare, Wicklow). For venues further afield (e.g., Cork, Galway, Kerry), a standard travel fee applies to cover fuel and crew time.
How do we secure the date? +
A booking deposit secures your date in our calendar. The balance is typically due closer to the wedding day or on the night, depending on the agreement.
