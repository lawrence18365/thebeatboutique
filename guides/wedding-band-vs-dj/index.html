Home / Guides / Wedding Band vs DJ

This is going to be an honest comparison. Yes, we're a wedding band, but we also provide DJ services and have worked alongside countless DJs. Both options have their place, and the right choice depends entirely on your priorities.

Quick Comparison

Factor Wedding Band DJ
Cost (Ireland) €2,400 - €4,000+ €400 - €1,200
Song variety Limited to repertoire (100-300 songs) Unlimited (any song ever recorded)
Atmosphere High energy, theatrical, immersive Consistent, crowd-pleasing
Space needed More (4-6 people + equipment) Less (1 person + DJ booth)
Sound levels Generally louder Easier to control
Breaks Need breaks between sets Continuous music

The Case for a Wedding Band

Pros

  • Creates a "wow" moment and talking point
  • Live energy that recordings can't replicate
  • Visual spectacle — becomes part of the entertainment
  • Can interact with crowd, take requests live
  • Unique first dance performance
  • Often includes DJ for breaks/late night

Cons

  • Significantly more expensive
  • Limited song selection
  • Requires more space
  • Need breaks between sets
  • Quality varies widely
  • Harder to preview (need to see live)

The Case for a DJ

Pros

  • Much more affordable
  • Can play any song ever recorded
  • Continuous music (no breaks)
  • Takes up less space
  • Easier to control volume
  • Can cater to very specific tastes

Cons

  • Less of a "moment" or talking point
  • No live performance energy
  • Guests may focus less on dancing
  • Quality varies enormously
  • Some venues prefer live music
  • Can feel less personal

When to Choose a Band

A wedding band makes most sense when:

  • Entertainment is a priority — You want the reception to feel like an event
  • You have the budget — And you'd rather spend on entertainment than, say, favours
  • Your venue suits it — Large ballroom, castle, or space with good acoustics
  • Your guests love to dance — Especially if you have an Irish or international crowd used to céilí-style energy
  • You want a unique first dance — A live performance is genuinely special

When to Choose a DJ

A DJ makes most sense when:

  • Budget is tight — You'd rather allocate funds elsewhere
  • You have specific music taste — Obscure genres, specific artists, or very eclectic mix
  • Space is limited — Intimate venues, restaurants, or outdoor settings
  • You want continuous music — No breaks, seamless mixing
  • Sound restrictions apply — Some venues have strict decibel limits that bands struggle with

The Hybrid Option

Many couples book a band for the main reception (2-3 hours) with DJ service before and after. This gives you the live energy for the peak of the night, with continuous music throughout. Most bands offer this as a package.

Questions to Help You Decide

Ask yourselves:

  1. What do you remember from weddings you've attended? Was it the band or the DJ that got people dancing?
  2. How important is the "entertainment factor"? Do you want a show, or background music that gets people moving?
  3. What's your crowd like? Will they dance to anything, or do they need to be won over?
  4. What's your venue like? Does it suit a 5-piece band or a more intimate setup?
  5. What's your real budget? Be honest about what you're comfortable spending on entertainment.

Our Honest Take

If you can afford a good band and your venue suits it, the live experience is hard to beat. But a great DJ will beat a mediocre band every time. Quality matters more than the format.

How to Choose Well (Either Option)

For Bands:

  • See them live before booking (showcases, public gigs, or weddings)
  • Check if the musicians you see are the ones who'll play your wedding
  • Read recent reviews, not just testimonials on their website
  • Ask about their experience at your venue

For DJs:

  • Meet them in person or video call — personality matters
  • Ask for sample mixes or playlists from past weddings
  • Check their equipment quality (especially speakers)
  • Discuss your music preferences in detail
