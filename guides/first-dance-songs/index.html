This guide shares the most requested first dance songs at Irish weddings, organised by style, plus practical tips for making your choice.

Tips for Choosing Your First Dance Song

1. Consider the Length

Most first dance songs are 3-4 minutes, but that can feel long when everyone's watching. We can shorten any song to 2-2.5 minutes, keeping the best parts and making the transition natural.

2. Think About the Lyrics

Listen carefully to the full lyrics. Some seemingly romantic songs have verses about breakups or loss. "Every Breath You Take" by The Police, for example, is actually quite dark when you pay attention.

3. Practice (A Little)

You don't need choreography, but dancing together a few times before the wedding helps. Even just swaying in your kitchen removes the awkwardness.

4. Consider a Live Version

A live band performance of your song adds intimacy and uniqueness. We can adjust the tempo, add personal touches, or create a medley if you can't choose between two songs.

5. It Doesn't Have to Be "Your Song"

If you don't have an obvious choice, pick something you both enjoy that sets the right mood. Many couples choose their first dance song specifically for the wedding rather than a pre-existing "our song."
