## Content Strategy: New Guide Pages

Prioritised by booking intent and keyword opportunity. Each guide should be 1,500-2,500 words, include internal links to relevant venue/location pages, and end with a CTA to check availability.

### Tier 1 — High Intent, Build Now

#### 1. Wedding Music Timeline
**Path:** `/guides/wedding-music-timeline/`
**Target keywords:** "wedding music timeline Ireland", "wedding day music schedule", "when does the band play at a wedding"
**Why:** No band competitor owns this. Currently dominated by photographers. We're the authority on when each music moment happens. Naturally walks couples through the full day and shows we handle every phase.
**Content angle:** Hour-by-hour breakdown from ceremony to last dance. Include: ceremony music (pre-processional, processional, signing, recessional), drinks reception, band setup window, dinner music, first dance timing, band sets, DJ handover. Add a downloadable timeline template.
**Internal links:** First dance songs guide, drinks reception guide, pricing guide, showcase

#### 2. Drinks Reception Music
**Path:** `/guides/drinks-reception-music/`
**Target keywords:** "drinks reception music Ireland", "wedding drinks reception ideas", "music for wedding drinks"
**Why:** Multiple competitors rank (drinksreceptionmusic.ie, irishweddingbands.ie, Picture Perfect). We offer DJ packages — this is a natural upsell. High search volume from couples who've booked a venue and are now planning entertainment.
**Content angle:** Options breakdown (solo acoustic, duo, DJ, playlist). What works at different venue types. Timing (typically 1-2 hours). Volume considerations. Our DJ package as a solution. Real examples from venues we've played.
**Internal links:** Venue pages (mention specific drinks reception setups), band vs DJ guide, pricing guide

#### 3. Wedding Entrance Songs
**Path:** `/guides/wedding-entrance-songs/`
**Target keywords:** "wedding entrance songs", "best entrance songs for wedding reception", "bride and groom entrance songs"
**Why:** Same format as our first dance songs page (which ranks #1-2). Proven template. Picture Perfect ranks for this — we can outrank them with our deeper credibility.
**Content angle:** 30-40 entrance songs categorised (dramatic, fun/upbeat, classic, modern). Include what the band can play live vs what works better as a track. Tips on coordinating with the MC. Real stories from weddings.
**Internal links:** First dance songs guide, song list page, showcase

#### 4. When to Book Your Wedding Band
**Path:** `/guides/when-to-book-wedding-band/`
**Target keywords:** "when to book wedding band Ireland", "how far in advance book wedding band", "wedding band booking timeline"
**Why:** Shortest guide to write. Highest booking intent — people searching this are ready to act. Multiple competitors have this but none rank strongly.
**Content angle:** Month-by-month availability data from our own bookings. Peak season (May-Oct) books 12-18 months ahead. Off-peak has more flexibility. What happens if you're last-minute. Red flags when bands are always available.
**Internal links:** Pricing guide, how to choose guide, showcase (to book)

### Tier 2 — High Volume, Build Next

#### 5. Average Wedding Cost Ireland (2026)
**Path:** `/guides/average-wedding-cost-ireland/`
**Target keywords:** "average wedding cost Ireland 2026", "how much does a wedding cost Ireland", "wedding budget breakdown Ireland"
**Why:** Massive search volume. Picture Perfect and 7 Entertainment rank for this. Trojan horse content — it's about weddings broadly but the entertainment section funnels directly to our pricing page. Establishes topical authority.
**Content angle:** Full budget breakdown (venue, catering, photography, entertainment, flowers, etc). Entertainment section goes deep: band (€1,500-€3,500), DJ (€600-€1,200), ceremony music (€300-€800), drinks reception (€400-€1,000). Position our pricing transparently. Include "where couples overspend" and "where to save" angles.
**Internal links:** Pricing guide, band vs DJ guide, venue pages

#### 6. Wedding Ceremony Music Guide
**Path:** `/guides/wedding-ceremony-music/`
**Target keywords:** "wedding ceremony songs Ireland", "church wedding music Ireland", "civil ceremony music Ireland"
**Why:** Every wedding needs ceremony music. Big keyword cluster. Picture Perfect has this page. Builds topical authority around "wedding music" — strengthens all our other pages.
**Content angle:** Church vs civil ceremony differences. The 4 key music moments (pre-ceremony, processional, signing of register, recessional). Song suggestions for each. Live musician vs recorded music. What we recommend from 500+ ceremonies. Irish traditional options vs modern.
**Internal links:** First dance songs, music timeline guide, song list

#### 7. Wedding Entertainment Ideas
**Path:** `/guides/wedding-entertainment-ideas/`
**Target keywords:** "wedding entertainment ideas Ireland", "unique wedding entertainment", "wedding reception entertainment"
**Why:** Broad keyword but high volume. Positions us in the market alongside all entertainment options. The Moogs, Picture Perfect, and Irish Wedding Bands all target this.
**Content angle:** Honest overview of all options — live band, DJ, silent disco, singing waiters, sax player, photo booth, lawn games. Pros/cons of each. What works at different venue sizes. How to combine (e.g. band + DJ package). Position ourselves as the premium band option without being salesy.
**Internal links:** Band vs DJ guide, corporate events, pricing guide

### Tier 3 — Niche but Valuable

#### 8. Wedding Day 2 Ideas
**Path:** `/guides/wedding-day-2-ideas/`
**Target keywords:** "wedding day 2 ideas Ireland", "day after wedding entertainment", "day 2 wedding Ireland"

#### 9. Best Wedding Party Songs / Floor Fillers
**Path:** `/guides/best-wedding-party-songs/`
**Target keywords:** "best wedding party songs", "songs to get guests dancing at wedding", "wedding floor fillers"
**Why:** Pure intent — couples searching this are deciding what music they want. Our 500+ weddings gives us real data on what actually fills a floor (vs what people think will).
**Content angle:** 50 songs that genuinely fill an Irish wedding dance floor, ranked by how consistently they work. Categories: guaranteed floor fillers, age-group crowd pleasers, Irish favourites, late-night singalongs. Include our live versions where possible.
**Internal links:** Song list, first dance songs, entrance songs

## Link Building (Off-Page)

Code can't fix this, but it's the biggest lever for moving from page 2 to page 1.

### Quick Wins
- **Wedding directories** — ensure listings on weddingsonline.ie, weddingdates.ie, onefabday.com with link to site
- **Venue websites** — ask venues you've played to add you to their "recommended suppliers" page (high-quality, relevant backlinks)
- **Google Business Profile** — optimise with photos, reviews, correct category, link to site
- **Supplier cross-linking** — photographers, videographers, celebrants you work with — link exchange on "partners" pages

### Medium-Term
- **Real wedding features** — submit real wedding stories to onefabday.com, weddingsonline.ie blog (they link back)
- **PR/media** — "500+ weddings" angle for local press, wedding magazines
- **Guest posts** — write for venue blogs about "choosing your wedding band" with link back

## Key Principles

1. **Write from experience, not research.** Every guide should reference "from 500+ weddings" and include real examples. This is our moat — directories can't do this.
2. **Every page earns or supports.** Guides earn traffic. Venue/location pages capture long-tail. Service pages convert. Every guide must link to at least one service page.
3. **One page per keyword cluster.** Don't cannibalise. "First dance songs" and "entrance songs" are separate pages. "Wedding band Dublin" and "Dublin wedding venues" are separate intents.
4. **Update annually.** Change year references (2026 -> 2027) every November. Google rewards freshness for wedding queries.
5. **Video embeds everywhere.** We have live performance footage. Embed it in guides. It increases time on page, reduces bounce, and differentiates from competitors publishing text-only AI content.
