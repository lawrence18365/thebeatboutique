Showcase FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes! The showcase is completely free. It's our way of letting you experience us before committing. There's no obligation to book - come, enjoy the music, and decide if we're right for you.

What if I want to book before the showcase?

Popular dates fill up fast. We offer a fully refundable deposit - secure your date now and see us at the showcase to confirm you love us. If you don't, we'll refund every cent.

Can I bring my partner/parents?

Absolutely! We encourage it. Bring anyone who's involved in the wedding music decision. The more the merrier - just let us know numbers when you RSVP.

What's the venue like?

Bell Bar in Blanchardstown has a great sound system and relaxed atmosphere. Free parking available. It's the perfect setting to see us in action without the wedding day nerves.

How long is the showcase?

The live performance is about 45 minutes, followed by Q&A and meet-the-band time. Most couples stay for about 2 hours total. Come and go as you please.

When's the next showcase after February?

We typically run showcases every 6-8 weeks during engagement season (October - April). Join our mailing list to be first to know about new dates.
