What Is an After-Band DJ Set?

The after-band set is the recorded-music part of your evening, beginning immediately after the live band. It gives you the impact of live musicians for the main reception and the range of a DJ for the finish. That means club favourites, current tracks, throwbacks, singalongs, and songs that would not naturally sit in a live band's repertoire can all become part of the same night.

It is not a separate event bolted onto the end. We treat the band and DJ as one continuous piece of entertainment. The running order, sound, and handover are planned together, with one clear goal: keep the room feeling full and the celebration moving.

The Handover: Live Band to DJ

The handover is where a combined package earns its keep. Because the DJ service is part of the same evening plan, the music can begin immediately after the band's final number. There is no second supplier arriving late, no unfamiliar setup being assembled in front of your guests, and no silence while people drift away from the dance floor.

We agree the running order with you and coordinate the practical details with your venue. On the night, the live set builds towards its finish and the DJ picks up the energy that is already in the room. The change feels natural because it has been considered before the first guest arrives.

Typical Wedding Timings

Most evenings follow a simple shape: your first dance opens the live portion, the band plays the main sets, and the DJ takes over after the final live song. From there, the DJ continues until the venue's permitted music finish time. We do not quote one universal curfew because each venue and date can be different.

Your final schedule depends on when dinner and speeches finish, how the venue runs the room, and the permitted finish time. If earlier parts of the day run late, the available entertainment window can become tighter. Our wedding music timeline shows how those parts fit together and where delays tend to affect the band and DJ.

Before the wedding, we confirm the plan with you and work with the venue on access, setup, and timing. You know when the live set is expected to finish, when the DJ begins, and what the venue allows at the end of the night.

Requests, Must-Plays, and Do-Not-Plays

Your preferences give the DJ a useful starting point. Send us the songs and artists you love, a small number of must-plays, and anything you definitely do not want. You do not need to build every minute of the set. A clear steer is usually more helpful than a rigid playlist because it leaves room to read the dance floor.

Guest requests can be considered on the night when they suit your brief and the mood in the room. The DJ balances those requests with what is working, changes direction when the crowd needs it, and avoids forcing a track simply because it appeared on a long list. The result still feels personal, but it can breathe like a real party.

After-Band DJ FAQ

Straight answers about the handover, timings, requests, and package.

What is an after-band DJ set?

An after-band DJ set begins as soon as the live performance ends and carries the celebration through the venue's permitted music finish time. It keeps the same dance floor moving with recorded tracks, requests, and a wider mix of styles.

Will there be a gap between the band and DJ?

No. When you book our band and DJ package, the live set is followed immediately by the professional DJ service. The handover is planned in advance so there is no dead air and guests have no reason to leave the dance floor.

How late does the after-band DJ play?

The DJ continues until the venue's permitted music finish time. That varies by venue and date, so we confirm the running order with you and the venue instead of promising a universal curfew.

Can we request songs for the DJ set?

Yes. Share your favourites, any must-plays, and any songs you do not want. Requests from guests can also be considered on the night when they fit the mood and your preferences.

Can we book the DJ with The Beat Boutique wedding band?

Yes. Most couples choose the full evening package, which combines the live band performance with the after-band DJ service. Your quote will reflect your date, venue, travel, finish time, and the package you choose.
