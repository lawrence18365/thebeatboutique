Showcase FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes! The showcase is completely free. It's our way of letting you experience us before committing. There's no obligation to book - come, enjoy the music, and decide if we're right for you.

What if I want to book before the showcase?

We offer a fully refundable booking fee (terms & conditions apply) - secure your date now and see us at the next showcase, to confirm you love us. If you don't, as long as you let us know within 2 days after the showcase date, we will refund your payment in full.

Can I bring my partner/parents?

Absolutely! We encourage it. Bring anyone who's involved in the wedding music decision. The more the merrier - just let us know numbers when you RSVP.

What's the venue like?

We host the showcase in a Dublin venue with great sound and easy parking. We'll confirm the exact venue details by email when the next date is announced.

How long is the showcase?

The showcase is a 2 hour show.

When's the next showcase?

We usually run showcases every 8 to 10 weeks throughout the year. Join our mailing list to be first to know about new dates.
