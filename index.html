Wedding Band Ireland FAQ

Straight answers to the questions Irish couples ask most.

How much does a wedding band cost in Ireland?

Most professional 4-5 piece bands in Ireland fall between €2,400 and €3,200 depending on date, travel, and add-ons. See our Pricing Guide.

Can we see you live before booking?

Yes — we run live showcases in Dublin so you can see us before booking.

Do you travel nationwide?

Yes. We perform across Ireland every week and handle all logistics with venues and planners.
