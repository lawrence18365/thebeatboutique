Wedding Culture in Tipperary

Tipperary weddings embrace the county's agricultural heritage with many farm and country house venues. Couples appreciate the value compared to more expensive counties while still enjoying high-quality venues. Weddings typically run 110-140 guests with a warm, community feel.
