Wedding Culture in Clare

Clare weddings range from castle grandeur at Dromoland to relaxed coastal celebrations. The county attracts many destination weddings and offers incredible natural backdrops. Weddings typically run 120-160 guests with couples appreciating both traditional and contemporary options.
