Wedding Culture in Meath

Meath has become Ireland's most popular wedding destination thanks to its concentration of stunning manor houses and country estates. Weddings typically run 140-180 guests with a focus on fine dining and dancing. The county's rich heritage adds a sense of occasion that couples love.
