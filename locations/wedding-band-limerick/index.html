Wedding Culture in Limerick

Limerick weddings often feature stunning manor houses and castles. Adare village alone draws couples from around the world. Weddings here tend toward the elegant end of the spectrum, with guest numbers ranging from 100-180 and a focus on sophisticated entertainment.
