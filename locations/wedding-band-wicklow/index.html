Known as the Garden County, Wicklow weddings often embrace the natural beauty with outdoor ceremonies and country house receptions. Weddings here tend to be slightly larger, averaging 130-160 guests, with couples seeking an elegant yet relaxed atmosphere that matches the mountainous backdrop.
