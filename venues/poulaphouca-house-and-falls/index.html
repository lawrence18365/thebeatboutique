About Poulaphouca House & Falls

Period house overlooking Blessington Lakes. With capacity for 140 guests, Poulaphouca House & Falls suits couples who want a scenic Blessington Lakes wedding with an outdoor feel, relaxed drinks reception energy, and a real party once everyone moves into the marquee.

Acoustics & Sound

The marquee suits a full live band well once the dancefloor is kept close to the stage. Because the setting is airy and scenic, we focus the PA carefully so speeches, first dance, and the evening sets still feel intimate rather than washed out.

Band Setup

The important part here is coordinating access to the grounds and marquee early so load-in stays tidy and out of the way of guests. Once we're in position, there's enough space for a proper band + DJ setup and a strong evening flow.

Insider Tip

If you're using the falls or lake views for photos, leave a little breathing room before the first dance so guests can settle back into the marquee before the music shifts into full evening mode.
