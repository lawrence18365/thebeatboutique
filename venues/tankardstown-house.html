Tankardstown House

Meath's Most Beloved Country House Venue

Wedding Band Guide: Tankardstown House

Tankardstown House is one of Ireland's most sought-after wedding venues, consistently rated among the country's top five. This beautifully restored Georgian manor house sits on 80 acres of picturesque Meath countryside, offering couples an exclusive-use experience with award-winning food and impeccable service. The Beat Boutique performs at Tankardstown more than any other venue in Meath - we know every corner of this magnificent estate.

The Orangery

The Orangery is Tankardstown's purpose-built reception space, and it's where the party really happens. The vaulted ceilings and exposed stonework create an intimate yet spacious atmosphere. The room has been designed with entertainment in mind - the stage area is well-positioned and the acoustics are superb. We've fine-tuned our sound for this space over many performances.

Pro Tip: The Orangery benefits from a slightly punchy bass response due to the stone walls. We adjust our EQ accordingly to keep the low end tight and clear without overwhelming the space.

What We've Learned at Tankardstown

  • Ceremony Music: The Woodland Church on the grounds is magical for outdoor ceremonies - we provide acoustic music that complements the natural setting
  • Drinks Reception: The Courtyard and Manor House are perfect for our acoustic duo or jazz trio
  • Load-In: Access to The Orangery is straightforward; the venue team are experienced and accommodating
  • Sound Limiters: Tankardstown has reasonable sound limits that we work within effortlessly
  • Local Knowledge: We arrive early and coordinate timing with the Tankardstown team who know us well
