About Ballymagarvey Village

Restored 18th century grain mill and village. With capacity for 250 guests, Ballymagarvey Village is couples who want rustic charm with modern convenience. the village setting is unique.

Acoustics & Sound

Converted barn with excellent natural sound. High ceilings and stone create warm acoustics.

Band Setup

Dedicated band area with good power. The venue team are very experienced.

Insider Tip

The courtyard between buildings creates a natural flow. We often see guests drifting between spaces all night.
