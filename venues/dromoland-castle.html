Wedding Band Guide: Dromoland Castle

Dromoland Castle has been the ancestral home of the O'Brien family, direct descendants of Brian Boru, High King of Ireland. Today, it's one of Ireland's most sought-after wedding venues, and The Beat Boutique has had the privilege of performing in its magnificent ballroom many times.

The Brian Boru Hall

The main reception space at Dromoland is the stunning Brian Boru Hall. With its Gothic Revival architecture, soaring ceilings, and incredible chandeliers, this room was designed for celebration. The acoustics are excellent - the stone walls provide natural warmth while the high ceilings prevent harsh reflections.

Pro Tip: Dromoland guests tend to be well-traveled and sophisticated. We always include some unexpected arrangements that show musical depth alongside the party anthems.

What We've Learned at Dromoland

  • Timing: The venue runs like clockwork - be ready to start exactly on schedule
  • Drinks Reception: The Drawing Room is perfect for our acoustic trio
  • Sound Levels: The wedding team are experienced and trust professional bands
  • Guest Mix: Often international guests - versatile setlists work best
