The Grand Ballroom Experience

Adare Manor isn't just a venue; it's a statement. Having performed at numerous weddings here, we understand that the entertainment needs to match the grandeur of the surroundings. The Grand Ballroom, with its sweeping staircase and riverside views, demands a performance that fills the room without overwhelming conversation.

💡 Pro Acoustics Tip

"The Grand Ballroom has high ceilings and glass surfaces. We use a distributed sound system specifically for this room to ensure the bass is tight and the vocals are crisp, preventing that 'muddy' echo often found in large ballrooms."

A Setlist for Adare
