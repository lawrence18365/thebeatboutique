Luttrellstown Castle

Dublin's Premier Castle Wedding Venue

Wedding Band Guide: Luttrellstown Castle

Luttrellstown Castle is one of Ireland's most exclusive wedding venues, famous for hosting the wedding of David and Victoria Beckham. Set within 600 acres of private parkland just 20 minutes from Dublin city center, this 15th-century castle offers a truly fairytale setting. The Beat Boutique has performed at Luttrellstown numerous times and understands the unique requirements of this magnificent venue.

The Ballroom

The castle's stunning ballroom is the main reception space, featuring soaring ceilings, elegant chandeliers, and large windows overlooking the estate. The room's natural acoustics are excellent, though we always bring our premium sound system to ensure every guest hears every note perfectly. The space accommodates up to 180 guests for a seated dinner.

Pro Tip: The Ballroom at Luttrellstown has a natural "live" sound that benefits from slightly reduced stage volume. We balance our mix accordingly to fill the room without overwhelming the space's elegant atmosphere.

What We've Learned at Luttrellstown

  • Ceremony Music: The Chapel or outdoor garden ceremonies call for acoustic arrangements - we've performed many first looks here
  • Drinks Reception: The Drawing Room and terrace are perfect for our acoustic duo or jazz trio during the canapé hour
  • Load-In: The venue team is highly experienced and professional; equipment access is well-coordinated
  • Guest Profile: Luttrellstown weddings often feature international guests who appreciate a mix of classic and contemporary hits
  • Exclusivity: As an exclusive-use venue, we have flexibility with setup times and sound checks
