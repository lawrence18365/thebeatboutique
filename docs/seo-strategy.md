# The Beat Boutique - SEO & Marketing Strategy

*Last Updated: January 2026*

---

## Executive Summary

The Beat Boutique is positioned as a premium "boutique" wedding band competing in a market of 1,200+ bands. Our key differentiators are:

1. **Fixed lineup** - "The guys in the video are the guys at your wedding"
2. **Free showcase events** - Risk elimination for couples
3. **Refundable deposit** - Until couples see us live
4. **183 five-star reviews** on WeddingsOnline

## Customer Psychology

### The Couple's Journey

| Stage | Timeline | Actions | Emotions |
|-------|----------|---------|----------|
| **Trigger** | 12-18 months out | Book venue, start Googling | Overwhelmed |
| **Research** | 9-12 months out | Watch videos, read reviews | Anxious |
| **Shortlist** | 6-9 months out | Contact 3-5 bands | Uncertain |
| **Validate** | Ongoing | Attend showcase or book blind | Seeking confidence |
| **Book** | 6-12 months out | Pay deposit | Relief |

### Core Fears

1. **"What if they're terrible on the day?"** - The #1 fear
2. **"Videos can be deceiving"** - Trust issues with online content
3. **"We've never done this before"** - First-time buyer anxiety
4. **"What if the dancefloor is empty?"** - Social embarrassment

### What Couples Want

1. **Packed dancefloor** - The ultimate success metric
2. **Social proof** - Reviews from real couples
3. **Risk elimination** - See band live before committing
4. **Personalization** - First dance, song requests
5. **Professionalism** - Smooth coordination with venue

### Key Decision Influencers

- Friends/family who recently married
- Venue wedding coordinators
- WeddingsOnline reviews
- Seeing band at another wedding
- Showcase attendance

**Tier 1 - High Volume, High Intent:**
- wedding band Ireland
- wedding band Dublin
- wedding band Cork
- wedding band Galway

**Tier 2 - Medium Volume, High Intent:**
- wedding band [county] (Kerry, Kildare, Wicklow, etc.)
