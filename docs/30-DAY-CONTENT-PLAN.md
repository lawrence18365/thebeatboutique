## Week 1: Foundation

### Monday (Day 1)
**Type:** Carousel (5 images)
**Topic:** "5 things to ask your wedding band before booking"
**Images:** Text-based graphics with your branding
**Caption:**
```
Choosing a wedding band? Ask these questions first 👇

1️⃣ "Will the same musicians be at my wedding as in your videos?"
(Some agencies swap musicians around - you deserve to know who's actually playing)

2️⃣ "Can I see you perform live before I book?"
(Any band worth their salt will invite you to a showcase)

3️⃣ "What's included in your price?"
(PA? Lighting? DJ? Travel? Get the full breakdown)

4️⃣ "What happens if someone's sick on the day?"
(Look for a real answer, not a dismissive one)

5️⃣ "Do you have public liability insurance?"
(Most venues require this - professionals have it)

Save this for when you're band shopping! 📌

#WeddingPlanning #IrishWedding #WeddingBandIreland #EngagedIreland #WeddingTips
```

## CONTENT CAPTURE CHECKLIST

At every wedding, film:

- [ ] Van packed / arrival shot
- [ ] Venue exterior (pretty establishing shot)
- [ ] Empty room before guests arrive
- [ ] Gear setup / sound check
- [ ] First dance (FULL SONG if possible)
- [ ] 3-5 dancefloor clips (different songs/moments)
- [ ] Crowd singalong moment
- [ ] High energy moment (hands in air, jumping)
- [ ] Last song of the night
- [ ] Quick "thank you" from couple (if they're up for it)

**Storage:** Create a folder for each wedding: `2026-01-25_SarahTom_AshfordCastle`

## POSTING TIMES

Best times for Irish wedding audience:
- **Weekdays:** 7-8pm (after work, scrolling on couch)
- **Sundays:** 10am-12pm (relaxed morning browsing)
- **Saturdays:** Avoid daytime (people are at weddings!)

Post consistently at the same times so followers know when to expect content.
