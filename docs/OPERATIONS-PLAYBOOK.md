# PART 2: INSTAGRAM CONTENT MACHINE

## Posting Schedule

| Day | Content Type | Example |
|-----|--------------|---------|
| Monday | Behind-the-scenes | "Load in at [Venue] today" - photo of gear being set up |
| Wednesday | Reel - Dancefloor moment | 15-30 sec clip of packed floor |
| Friday | Reel - Song clip or first dance | Full song or emotional moment |
| Saturday | Stories from tonight's gig | Real-time stories during wedding |
| Sunday | Carousel - Wedding recap | "Last night at [Venue]" with 5-6 photos |

## Content Bank (Film at Every Wedding)

Capture these at EVERY gig:
1. **The setup shot** - Empty room with gear ready
2. **Sound check clip** - 10 seconds of any song
3. **First dance** - Full song if possible, or best 60 seconds
4. **Packed dancefloor** - Multiple 15-second clips from different angles
5. **Crowd reaction shot** - Guests singing along, hands in air
6. **End of night** - "Last song" energy
7. **Couple thank you** - If they're up for it, 10-second video saying they loved it

### Permission System

Add to your booking contract:
```
PHOTO & VIDEO CONSENT

We occasionally share clips and photos from weddings on our social media
(Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). These help future couples see what we do.

Please tick your preference:

[ ] Yes, you can share photos/videos from our wedding
[ ] Yes, but please don't show our faces
[ ] No, please don't share any content from our wedding

Signature: _______________ Date: _______________
```

## Reel Formulas That Work

### Formula 1: The Transformation
- First 2 seconds: Empty dancefloor
- Text overlay: "POV: The band starts playing Mr. Brightside"
- Cut to: Packed dancefloor going mental
- Duration: 8-15 seconds
- Audio: Original song

### Formula 2: The Reaction
- Film yourself walking into a venue
- Text: "Walking into Ashford Castle for sound check"
- Show the venue reveal
- Duration: 10-20 seconds

### Formula 3: The First Dance
- Full first dance or best 60 seconds
- Text overlay with couple's names (with permission)
- Emotional, high-quality audio
- Caption: "When [Song Name] hits different at a wedding"

### Formula 4: The Request
- Text: "When the groom secretly requests this for his bride"
- Play the song
- Show bride's reaction if you captured it
- Emotional payoff

# PART 5: SHOWCASE OPTIMIZATION

## Before the Showcase

### 2 Weeks Before
- Email all enquiries from last 3 months who haven't booked
- Post on Instagram with countdown
- Email past showcase attendees who didn't book

### 1 Week Before
- Reminder email to all RSVPs
- Instagram story countdown
- Confirm numbers with venue

### Day Before
- Final reminder email with address, parking, what to expect
- Check all gear
- Print materials (see below)

## At the Showcase

### Materials to Have
1. **Sign-up sheet** - Name, email, wedding date, venue
2. **Business cards** - Simple, with QR code to website
3. **One-page info sheet** - Packages, what's included, pricing range
4. **Contract + pen** - For same-night bookings
5. **Card reader** - Take deposits on the spot

### The Ask
At the end of the set, Pete speaks:

```
"Thanks so much for coming out tonight. If you've got any questions,
we'll be hanging around for the next hour - come grab us.

If you're ready to lock in your date, we can do that tonight.
We've got our diaries here and can check availability right now.

And for anyone who books tonight, we'll knock €150 off as a thank you
for coming to see us in person. That offer's just for tonight."
```

## After the Showcase

### Same Night
- Collect all sign-up sheets
- Note who booked / who was "very interested" / who was "just looking"
- Add everyone to email list

### Next Day
Send personalized email to every attendee:

**For "Very Interested" (asked lots of questions, mentioned specific date):**
```
Subject: Great to meet you last night!

Hey [Names],

So lovely to meet you both at the showcase last night!

I've just checked and [their date] is still available. Given how quickly our 2026 dates are going, I wanted to let you know before it's gone.

If you want to secure it, I can send over the booking form today. Remember, the €150 showcase discount is valid until [3 days from now].

Any questions at all, just shout.

Pete
```

**For "Just Looking" (early in planning, no set date):**
```
Subject: Lovely to meet you!

Hey [Names],

Thanks for coming along to the showcase last night! Hope you enjoyed it.

When you've got your date and venue locked in, give us a shout and we'll check availability. No rush - I've added you to our mailing list so you'll hear about future showcases too.

Best of luck with the planning!

Pete
```

### Day 3
Follow up with anyone who was "very interested" but hasn't booked:

```
Subject: Quick check-in

Hey [Names],

Just wanted to follow up - have you had a chance to think about the band?

The showcase discount (€150 off) expires tonight, so wanted to make sure you didn't miss it if you were planning to book.

No pressure either way - let me know if you have any questions.

Pete
```

# PART 9: FINANCIAL TARGETS

## Annual Revenue Model

Assuming average booking of €3,000:

| Weddings | Revenue |
|----------|---------|
| 40 weddings | €120,000 |
| 50 weddings | €150,000 |
| 60 weddings | €180,000 |

## Booking Pipeline

To book 50 weddings, assuming 25% enquiry-to-booking rate:
- Need 200 enquiries/year
- That's ~17 enquiries/month
- From: WeddingsOnline (8), Google/Website (4), Instagram (2), Referrals (2), Showcases (1)

## Capacity Planning

Maximum sustainable: ~50-55 weddings/year (mostly weekends, May-September heavy)

Peak season (May-Sep): ~6-8 weddings/month capacity
Off-season (Oct-Apr): ~2-4 weddings/month capacity
